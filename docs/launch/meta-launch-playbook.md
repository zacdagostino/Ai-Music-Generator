# Meta Launch Playbook (First 30 Days)

## Campaign Structure
- Campaign A: In Memory
- Campaign B: In Love
- Objective: Purchase conversion
- Budget: $30-$50/day total

## Creative Angles
### In Memory
- Gift for parent/sibling remembrance
- Anniversary of passing
- Personal closure and tribute

### In Love
- Anniversary surprise
- Wedding/vow extension
- Inside-story love song

## Constraints
- Calm premium tone only
- No urgency gimmicks or countdowns
- No "instant AI" framing

## Optimization Cadence (2x weekly)
- Pause creative sets with CTR < 0.8% for 5 consecutive days
- Reallocate spend to highest CVR collection+tier pair
- Feed top-performing language into landing and checkout copy

## Success Signal
- 10 paid orders in 30 days
- AOV >= $200
- At least 1 paid order from each collection
